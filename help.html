AmateurFriction

Turn your urgent deadline into an organized plan.


What is this?

AmateurFriction is a website that helps procrastinators organize their time to feel more productive.
In depth, This is an AI that can read through your provided information, analyse it, and help you by giving you each time stamp and what you are going to do in it! It is originally inspired by the last minute exam stress, the one most of us get when we want to study last minute but don't know if we even have enough time or a good enough plan, but we always do, with AmateurFriction's help everyone will be thriving


How does it work?

By using the help of openAI and algorithms, all you have to do is simply provide the necessary information needed, and you will get your plan, please make sure the time and task is realistic enough for the best results possible!
